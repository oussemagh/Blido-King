🎁 Gifts guide

How gifts work

  • Every 4 rounds, two 🎁 boxes appear on random free squares of the white track (never on stars).
  • Land on a box to get a random gift. You have two slots: one active gift (you use it) and one passive gift (it works by itself, like 5omsa). If the slot a gift needs is full, the box just vanishes.
  • Each player has a gift bar under the board, always visible, with an Active slot and a Passive slot. Tap your Active gift on your turn to use it. The small ✕ throws a gift away.
  • If several tokens can use it, they glow: tap the one you want. Tap the gift again to cancel.
  • The % next to each gift is its chance per box. If a slot is full, that gift is skipped and the others share its chance.
  • Capture a token while your hands are empty and its owner holds a gift: you steal it, slot by slot. If you both hold a gift in the same slot, his is destroyed.

The gifts

🫒
Zit Zitouna
Spills oil on the square of your chosen token. Any token stepping onto it, yours too, slips, flips and stops there even with steps left. One slip per spill.
🌶️
Felfel
Burns the opponent token directly in front of yours: he eats the pepper, turns red and returns to his yard. Not on star squares.
🫘
Loubia
Gasses the opponent token directly behind yours with a green fart and sends it back to its yard. Not on star squares.
🧿
3in
Curses all other players: dice only show 1 to 3 for their next two turns (👁️). A player holding 5omsa is protected and loses it.
🪬
5omsa
Passive gift (goes in your passive slot): blocks one 3in, then breaks. While held it also cancels a curse you already have.
🧹
Msal7a
From the white lane, beside an opponent token inside his colored lane: sweep him out onto the white-lane square on the opposite side. He loses his lane progress.
🩴
Chleka Bou Sbo3
Throw it at an opponent token up to 3 squares ahead of one of yours: it can’t move on its next turn and has stars over its head. Not on star squares.
Chabb
Blinds the opponent tokens 1 or 2 squares ahead of or behind your token for 3 of their owner’s turns (🕶️). A blind token can’t enter its colored lane: it walks past and makes another full lap.
